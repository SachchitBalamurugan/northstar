Course description
CS 61B covers the design and analysis of data structures: lists, trees, hash tables, heaps, graphs, sorting, and the software engineering habits needed to build a large program on your own. Programming is in Java.

Grading: Projects 40%, Homework 15%, Labs 10%, Midterms 15% (two midterms, 7.5% each), Final exam 20%. Letter grades follow fixed cutoffs (A 90%, B 80%, C 70%); the course is not curved.

Late policy: Every student has 5 slip days for the semester, with at most 2 used on any one project or homework. After slip days run out, late work loses 10% per day and is not accepted more than 3 days late. Labs cannot be turned in late. Slip days cannot be used on exams.

Extensions: If you have a DSP accommodation or a serious personal situation, fill in the extension form on the course website at least 24 hours before the deadline. You do not need to explain private details.
