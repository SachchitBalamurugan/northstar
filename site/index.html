How it works

Three steps.
You stay in charge.

01

Connect Google

Sign in with your Google account so Northstar can see your Gmail and Google Calendar.

02

Northstar reads and plans

It sorts emails, events, approvals and tasks, then puts the one thing that matters most at the top.

03

You approve

Drafts, events and applications wait for your OK. Nothing is sent or submitted without you.
